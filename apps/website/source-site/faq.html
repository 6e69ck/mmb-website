FAQ for Students

1. What does a marching band do exactly?

We perform at a ton of events throughout the school year. Every year we'll play Santa Claus Parades and varsity sports games, as well as other events that come our way, such as Toronto Raptors Half-Time shows or a New Year's Eve Concert with Serena Ryder.

2. How much are membership fees? 

There is no annual membership fee! All members are required to pay a one-time $40 fee and a $100 deposit for insurance, but the fee can be returned if you decide not to join. The insurance is also returned to you at the end of the year provided that your assigned uniform and instrument is undamaged.

3. Do I have to bring my own instrument? 

No! MMB has a wide variety of brass, woodwind, and percussion instruments that are free for our members.

4. Do I have to pay for my uniform? 

Nope! MMB has uniforms of all sizes and you'll be assigned a full set before your first event.

5. When are rehearsals?

 MMB meets Tuesdays 7:30-10:00 PM in Fitzhenry Studio, David Braley Athletic Centre (DBAC). More information can be found on our join page.
6. I have another question!
We'd love to hear it – drop us a line and we'll get back to you!
 

FAQ for Event Organizers

1. Can we hire MMB for an event?
To get the ball rolling to hire MMB, get in touch with your event details!
2. How much does hiring MMB cost?
It depends based on the event, date, and location. Drop us a line and we can provide a quote.
3. How many performers do you have?
We typically perform with 40+ musicians. If you have a specific headcount in mind, let us know and we'd be happy to explore the possibilities with you!
